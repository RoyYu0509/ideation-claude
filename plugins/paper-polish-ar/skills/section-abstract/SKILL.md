# Abstract and Title

Heuristics and acceptance criteria for the title and abstract (funnel logic, the sentence-function skeleton, a grabby title, 1-2 key quantitative results).

- The full rubric (the checkable acceptance criteria, with verdict + evidence per item) is in
  `references/rubric.md`. Read it before writing or scoring this section.
- Model passages and before/after pairs specific to this section live in `house-style/examples/venue-anchors/`.

## Writing register for this section

**Abstract = compression, not expansion.** Every sentence must be the sharpest summary-level
statement the paper already supports.

- **Condense; do not introduce.** No assumption, sub-component, parameter, or qualifier should
  appear here that is not already established in the paper body. If the detail is new to this
  sentence, it does not belong in the abstract.
- **Summary-level language only.** The unit is "what was found / done / shown" — not "how exactly,
  under what setting, using which variant." Strip specificity below the level of the core finding.
  One or two key quantitative metrics are required; sub-method components, dataset details, and
  hyperparameters are not.
- **Four moves, ~5–7 sentences.** Background → gap → core finding → significance. If a revision is
  adding sentences beyond this, that is a signal the direction is wrong — cut, do not add.
- **Do not add hedges the paper does not require.** "May," "could potentially," "seems to suggest"
  must reflect the paper's actual claim strength. Do not insert softening language as a precaution;
  that weakens claims the paper earns.
