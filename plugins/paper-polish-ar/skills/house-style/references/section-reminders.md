# Section reminders (shared across all section-* skills)

These three reminders apply to every section when revising or evaluating.

- **Freeze the science.** You may rewrite this section freely, but never change data, numbers,
  claims, claim strength, citations, symbols, or the topic. Edit in proportion to need. (The
  canonical, detailed freeze boundary — caption attribution, scope clauses, gray-area science calls
  to escalate — is in the `polish-prose` skill's "## The freeze boundary"; this is the summary.)
- **Whole-paper context.** This section must cohere with the rest of the paper in voice,
  terminology, and transitions: read the full manuscript first.
- **Structure latent, not visible.** Apply the structural logic in the rubric without letting the
  template seams show (see the house-style skill note on the core tension).
