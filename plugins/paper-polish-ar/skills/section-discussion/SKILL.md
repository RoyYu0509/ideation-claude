# Discussion and Conclusion

Heuristics and acceptance criteria for the Discussion/Conclusion (significance, advance, mechanism, honest limits, a restrained closing).

- The full rubric (the checkable acceptance criteria, with verdict + evidence per item) is in
  `references/rubric.md`. Read it before writing or scoring this section.
- Model passages and before/after pairs specific to this section live in `house-style/examples/venue-anchors/`.

## Writing register for this section

- **Significance grounded in specific findings.** State what changes for the field *because of
  exactly these results* — not a general statement about the field's importance. Anchor every
  significance claim to a specific result from this paper.
- **Limitations as boundary conditions, not hedges.** "This approach may not generalize" is a
  hedge. "This approach requires X and fails when Y because Z" is an honest limit. Be specific:
  name the condition and the reason.
- **Reframe a weak result, don't hedge it.** When a result reads weaker than its claim, retrieve
  `reframe-result` and find the honest framing (`null_positive` / `effect_ratio` / `scope_shrink`) —
  present the smaller result as a real bound, a ratio, or a claim scoped to the regime it supports —
  before you reach for a soften.
- **Do not re-list results.** Discussion explains meaning; Results reports numbers. If a sentence
  re-states a number without adding interpretive content, cut or reframe it.
