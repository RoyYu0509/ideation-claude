# Discussion and Conclusion — rubric

Acceptance criteria for the Discussion/Conclusion. Verdict + evidence per item. First explain the
meaning of the results, then close the paper. Does not repeat Results technical detail.

**Few-shot** (a hyped, number-re-listing, limit-free draft → the polished, sober contribution+meaning

## Items
1. **Meaning.** What the results say and why they matter, framed around the core finding.
2. **Advance.** How the work moves past existing methods.
3. **Mechanism / reason.** The mechanism or theoretical reason behind the findings.
4. **Limits.** Honest applicability conditions and limitations.
5. **Future.** Concrete directions the work can be extended.
6. **Closing.** A concise final paragraph that states the core contribution and its meaning for a
   broader class of scientific problems, technical routes, or method frameworks.

## Tone (use as overall verdict)
Restrained, clear, and judicious — conveys the contribution while honestly stating boundaries; no
exaggeration, no empty generalities.

## Anti-patterns (flag)
- Repeating Results detail or re-listing numbers.
- Over-claiming significance / generality the paper does not support.
- A vague, grand closing with no specific contribution.
- Hiding or omitting limitations.
