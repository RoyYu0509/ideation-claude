# Results

Heuristics and acceptance criteria for the Results (a finding-centered, progressive evidence chain rather than a figure-ordered experiment log).

- The full rubric (the checkable acceptance criteria, with verdict + evidence per item) is in
  `references/rubric.md`. Read it before writing or scoring this section.
- Model passages and before/after pairs specific to this section live in `house-style/examples/venue-anchors/`.

## Writing register for this section

- **Lead with the finding, not the experiment.** "The method achieves X under conditions Y" — not
  "We ran experiment Z and Fig. N shows…" The result sentence opens the claim; evidence follows.
- **Numbers are mandatory for core claims.** A comparison stated without a quantity ("faster,"
  "more accurate," "lower error") is a placeholder, not a result. Fill it with the number or cut
  the sentence.
- **Interpret; do not over-project.** Explain what each result means for the paper's core claim.
  Do not extend the implication beyond what the data show; that belongs in Discussion.
- **Reframe a weak / borderline result before softening it.** When a finding reads weaker than the
  claim around it, retrieve `reframe-result` and find the honest, more-correct framing (state the
  empirical effect directly, restate as a ratio / CI bound, shrink scope) before you reach for a hedge.
