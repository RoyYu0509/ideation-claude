## Writing register for this section

- **The gap must be specific.** Name the exact mechanism, capability, or empirical finding prior
  work lacks — not "this area is underexplored" or "no work has addressed." A vague gap produces a
  vague contribution.
- **Contributions are claims, not aspirations.** State only what this paper actually establishes, at
  the strength the evidence supports. Do not write contribution statements that are stronger than
  the Results section can back.
- **Significance framed for the field, not the author.** "Researchers in [field] have long been
  limited by [X]" — not "we study [X] because it is important / interesting." The field's need
  motivates the work, not the author's interest in it.
